02 / WHY TWO CHECKS MATTER

A matching number can still make the wrong claim.

NUMERICAL MATCHING

50/50 · 5-year windows

Both figures appear in the source. That does not turn a historical observation into a promise about the future.

HIGH · DELIBERATELY SEEDED ERROR

“A hypothetical 50/50 US/European buyout blend guarantees positive returns over every 5-year window.”

The source describes historical outcomes of a hypothetical blend. Those observations cannot establish a guarantee of future returns.

Suggested correction: “Over the 30-year period examined, a hypothetical 50/50 US/European buyout blend did not generate negative annualised returns over any 5-year rolling window in either USD or EUR terms.”

RM briefing
## In one line
The Passport paper examines how geographic diversification can affect the behaviour of US and European buyout exposures, while emphasising the limitations of historical evidence.

## Why it matters to clients
A hypothetical 50/50 US/European buyout blend guarantees positive returns over every 5-year window.

## Three talking points
1. Over the 30-year period examined, a hypothetical 50/50 US/European buyout blend did not generate negative annualised returns over any 5-year rolling window in either USD or EUR terms. It is an index-level illustration with quarterly rebalancing, not an actual fund or recommended allocation. Valuation smoothing can affect reported private equity returns.
2. From 30 September 1995 to 30 September 2025, US buyout's average rolling 5-year correlation with the S&P Global 1200 was 0.82, compared with 0.56 for European buyout. These historical relationships are not guarantees of future diversification.
3. The first- to third-quartile manager spread was approximately 19 percentage points in 10-year IRR in both buyout geographies, based on closed-end funds as of 30 September 2025. This is a percentage-point difference, not percentage outperformance.

## Handle with care
- The guarantee above is deliberately incorrect, to illustrate the review process. The source reports historical outcomes only.
- The blend is hypothetical and uses quarterly rebalancing at index level.
- Private equity valuations can lag public markets. Reported stress-period returns may reflect smoothing.
- Past performance is not a guide to future results.

## Useful conversations
- How the paper defines and measures geographic diversification.
- Why a historical absence of negative rolling returns cannot establish future protection.
- How manager dispersion differs from an allocation-level return comparison.

04 / REVIEW AND RELEASE

The person stays in control.

  1. Check each selected finding against its supporting passage and context; edit or deselect it if needed.
  2. Mark each selected finding as reviewed, then approve the set for drafting.
  3. Review numerical matches, meaning flags and audience-specific wording. An incomplete AI review blocks sign-off.
  4. Sign off the exact version only after reviewing it. Revisions to findings require regenerated drafts and fresh approval.
  5. Download the review pack before leaving. Work is not currently saved between sessions.
This is an on-demand editorial workflow. Scheduled ingestion, persistent review queues and distribution tracking are future extensions, not current features.
