02 / WHY TWO CHECKS MATTER

A matching number can still make the wrong claim.

NUMERICAL MATCHING

USD 2.1 trillion · 2023

Both figures appear in the source. That alone does not establish what they measure.

HIGH · DELIBERATELY SEEDED ERROR

“Global private credit invested assets exceeded USD 2.1 trillion in 2023.”

The source includes assets and committed capital. Calling the whole measure invested assets changes its meaning.

Suggested correction: “Global private credit assets and committed capital exceeded USD 2.1 trillion in 2023.”

This flag is a prepared illustration. In a live run, numerical matching is performed by code and a separate AI review checks meaning. Neither replaces a person's assessment of the source.

Six prepared outputs to explore.

RM briefing
## In one line
The IMF's historical private-credit assessment highlights the importance of definitions, valuation practices and the date of a risk assessment.

## Why it matters to clients
Global private credit invested assets exceeded USD 2.1 trillion in 2023.

## Talking points
- Infrequent trading and model-based valuations can delay recognition of deterioration. Low reported price volatility does not necessarily mean low economic risk.
- At the time of its April 2024 assessment, the IMF saw limited immediate financial-stability risks but warned that growth, opacity and interconnectedness could increase vulnerabilities.

## Handle with care
- These are historical observations, not a current market update.
- The scale measure includes committed capital. The deliberately incorrect description above illustrates why matching a number alone is insufficient.

## Useful conversations
- Understanding how a source defines its market-size measure.
- Distinguishing reported price volatility from economic risk.
- Keeping the original date and qualifications attached to risk assessments.

The summary also explains why low reported price volatility does not necessarily mean low economic risk. Infrequent trading and model-based valuations can delay recognition of deterioration. A smooth reported valuation series should therefore be read alongside the source's qualifications.

These points provide background for interpreting the research, rather than a recommendation about an allocation or a product. The source gives further context for its definitions and observations.

04 / REVIEW AND RELEASE

The person stays in control.

  1. Check each selected finding against its supporting passage and context; edit or deselect it if needed.
  2. Mark each selected finding as reviewed, then approve the set for drafting.
  3. Review numerical matches, meaning flags and audience-specific wording. An incomplete AI review blocks sign-off.
  4. Sign off the exact version only after reviewing it. Revisions to findings require regenerated drafts and fresh approval.
  5. Download the review pack before leaving. Work is not currently saved between sessions.
This is an on-demand editorial workflow. Scheduled ingestion, persistent review queues and distribution tracking are future extensions, not current features.
